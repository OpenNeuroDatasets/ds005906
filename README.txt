Date: April 4th, 2025

This dataset repository contains de-identified fMRI and T1-weighted imaging acquired from 16 Parkinson's disease patients (aged 50-76 years) receiving deep brain stimulation therapy. All patients were implanted in the subthalamic nucleus or globus pallidus internus.
